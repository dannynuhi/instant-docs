Formyro is a document utility project. This page explains the safe monetization posture for advertising, affiliate links, and partnerships.

Current status

  • Formyro is online and publicly accessible.
  • Advertising scripts are not added unless advertising approval is verified.
  • Affiliate links are not added unless program details are verified.
  • Revenue, traffic, sponsor, and user claims are not made unless supported by real data.

Advertising policy

If advertising is added in the future, Formyro should preserve user trust, avoid accidental clicks, avoid deceptive ad placement, and keep primary document actions easy to use.

Affiliate and partnership policy

Any affiliate or partnership placement must be useful, clearly disclosed, and based on verified program details. Formyro does not publish fake rankings, fake reviews, or unsupported performance claims.

Privacy posture

Monetization work should remain privacy-safe and avoid collecting unnecessary personal information.
