Rental Application Letter

A letter to support a rental application.

When to use this

Use this template when you need a clear, organized starting point that saves time while still letting you customize the final wording for your situation.

Copy-ready template

Dear [Landlord/Manager],\n\nI am applying to rent [Property]. I am prepared to provide [Documents] and can move in on [Date]. Thank you for considering my application.\n\nSincerely,\n[Your Name]

How to customize it

Replace bracketed text with your real details. Keep the final version specific, brief, and accurate. Remove any section that does not apply.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Do not leave bracketed placeholder text in the final version.
  • Do not add fake dates, fake credentials, fake claims, or unsupported details.
  • Do not send the first draft without proofreading names, dates, addresses, and numbers.

FAQ

Yes. This is designed as a free starting point.

Should I edit it?

Yes. The best version is always personalized to the real situation.
